# Reading positions, iCloud and Handoff

## Pick up where you left off

VectorPDF remembers the last page and zoom for each document, and returns to them when you open it again.

## Sync with your other Macs

If you're signed in to iCloud, your reading position and [anchors](anchors.md) follow you to your other Macs with the same Apple Account. This works for files in iCloud Drive and cloud-storage folders, even if the folder is in a different location on each Mac.

Your documents and their text aren't uploaded. To keep this information on your Mac, turn off **Sync reading positions with iCloud** in [Settings](settings.md). That also deletes VectorPDF's entries from iCloud.

> **Note:** iCloud sync depends on how a copy of VectorPDF was built, so it may not be available if you built VectorPDF yourself.

## Handoff

With Handoff turned on, you can continue reading on another Mac. VectorPDF offers the document and page you're on, and opens them if the file is available on that Mac.

To turn Handoff on or off, see the settings for AirDrop & Handoff in System Settings.
