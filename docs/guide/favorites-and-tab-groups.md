# Favorites and Tab Groups

## Favorites

Favorites keep the PDFs you use most within easy reach.

- **Add or remove the current PDF:** choose Favorites > Add This PDF to Favorites, or press ⌘D. Press ⌘D again to remove it.
- **Open a favorite:** choose it in the Favorites menu, then choose Open, or click it on the Start screen.

The same commands appear when you right-click the document's title or an empty part of the toolbar, along with your Favorites and Tab Groups.

## Tab Groups

A Tab Group is a named set of documents that open together as tabs of one window, such as the specifications for a project.

### Save a group

1. Open the documents as tabs in one window.
2. Choose Favorites > Save All Open Tabs as a Group.
3. Enter a name, then click Save.

### Open a group

Choose the group in the Favorites menu, then choose Open, or click it on the Start screen.

### Change a group

Open the group, add or close tabs, then choose Favorites > Update Group with Open Tabs.

### Delete a group

Choose the group in the Favorites menu, then choose Delete Tab Group. If you delete a group or remove a favorite from the Start screen, you can click Undo in the message that appears.
