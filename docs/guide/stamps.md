# Stamps and signatures

A stamp is an image, such as your signature or initials, that you can place on any page.

> **Note:** A stamp is a visual mark. It isn't a cryptographic digital signature, and it doesn't certify the document.

## Create your stamp

1. Choose VectorPDF > Settings, then click Create Stamp under Digital Stamp. You can also just choose the Stamp tool in the Markup toolbar, and VectorPDF asks you to create one.
2. Choose how to make it:
   - **Draw:** sign with your trackpad or mouse.
   - **Type:** enter your name or initials, and choose a font.
   - **Image:** choose a PNG or JPEG file. If you choose **Transparent background**, the page shows through the background of the image.
3. Click Use.

VectorPDF keeps your stamp on this Mac for next time. To replace or remove it, use Modify Stamp or Delete Stamp in [Settings](settings.md).

## Place a stamp

1. Show the Markup toolbar (⇧⌘A), then choose the Stamp tool.
2. Click where you want the stamp.

Then drag it to move it, or drag a corner to resize it. To remove it, select it and press Delete, or right-click it and choose Delete Signature.

## Sign a form

If a form has a signature field, click **Sign** in the field. See [Fill in forms](forms.md).
