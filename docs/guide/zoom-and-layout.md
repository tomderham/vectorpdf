# Zoom, rotate and page layout

## Zoom

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom in or out | Press ⌘+ or ⌘−, click + or − in the toolbar, or pinch on a trackpad |
| Set an exact zoom | Click the percentage in the toolbar, type a value from 10 to 1000, then press Return |
| See the page at actual size | Choose View > Actual Size (100%), or press ⌘1 |
| Fit the page's width | Choose View > Zoom to Fit Width, or press ⌘9 |
| Fit the whole page | Choose View > Zoom to Fit Window, or press ⌥⌘0 |

The Zoom In and Zoom Out commands work between 25% and 400%. Type a percentage to go beyond those limits.

VectorPDF keeps pages sharp at every zoom, because it redraws them rather than enlarging an image.

## What 100% means

Choose View > Define 100% Scale As, then choose one:

- **Size on Screen Equals Size on Printout:** a page at 100% appears at about its printed size.
- **1 Point Equals 1 Screen Point:** one PDF point takes up one point on your display.

## Rotate the view

Click Rotate Left or Rotate Right in the toolbar. This only changes how you see the document on screen, and isn't saved. To rotate pages in the PDF itself, see [Rearrange pages](pages.md).

## Two-Page Mode

Click Two-Page Mode in the toolbar, or choose View > Two Pages (⌥⌘2). Pages appear in pairs with the cover page on its own, like an open book, and the spread is zoomed to fit the window. To go back, click the button again or choose View > Single Page.

> **Note:** While the view is rotated or in Two-Page Mode, you can't select text, follow links, use the markup and measurement tools, or fill in forms. Return to Single Page with no rotation to use them.
