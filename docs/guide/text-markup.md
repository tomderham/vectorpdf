# Highlight, underline and strike through

## Mark up selected text

Select text, then do one of these:

| Markup | Menu | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Highlight | Edit > Highlight Selection | ⇧⌘H |
| Underline | Edit > Underline Selection | ⇧⌘U |
| Strikethrough | Edit > Strikethrough Selection | ⇧⌘X |

You can also use the buttons in the Markup toolbar, or right-click the selection and choose Highlight, Underline or Strikethrough. Each has a list of colors.

## Choose a color

The Markup toolbar (⇧⌘A) has a color button. Click it, then click a color. The shortcuts and toolbar buttons use the color selected there. To pick a color for one markup, use the color list in the right-click menu, or under the item in the Edit menu.

## Remove a markup

To take back a markup you just made, choose Edit > Undo or press ⌘Z.

Right-click the marked text, then choose Remove Highlight, Remove Underline or Remove Strikethrough. Or choose the Eraser in the Markup toolbar, then click or drag across the markups you want to remove. ⌘Z brings back a markup you removed by mistake.

## Summarize your markups

To get a list of your annotations, choose File > Export Annotations Summary (⇧⌘E). You can save it as Markdown or CSV. See [Export to other formats](export.md).
