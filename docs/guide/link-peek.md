# Preview links with Link Peek

Technical documents are full of references: “see Equation 3”, “as shown in Figure 2”, “[14]”. Link Peek shows you the target where you are, so you don't lose your place.

## Peek at a link

- Hold the pointer over a link, then **hold ⌘**. Let go to close the preview.
- Or **Force Click** the link on a Force Touch trackpad. The preview closes when you release.

The preview shows what's useful for the kind of target: a few lines for an equation, the opening paragraph for a section, and the top of a table or figure.

## Follow a link

Click a link to go to its target. Choose Go > Back (⌘[) to return.

Web links open in your browser. You can choose which one with the [Open Links In](settings.md) setting.

## Open the target in a new window

To keep the target on screen beside your page:

- **⌘-click** the link.
- Or right-click the link, then choose **Open in New Window**.

You can also right-click text, or a place on the page, to open that location in a new window. See [Tabs and windows](tabs-and-windows.md#anchor-windows).

## Keep the target

Right-click a link, then choose **Create Anchor** to save its target as an [anchor](anchors.md), or **Create Anchor and Open in New Window** to do both.
