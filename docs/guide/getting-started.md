# Get started with VectorPDF

VectorPDF needs macOS 15 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon. Drag it to your Applications folder to install it.

## Open your first PDF

When VectorPDF starts without a document, it shows the Start screen. Do any of these:

- Drag a PDF onto the drop zone.
- Click the drop zone, then choose a file.
- Press ⌘O.

You can also open a PDF from the Finder with [Open With](open-documents.md), or choose one from Tab Groups, Favorites or Recent Documents on the Start screen.

## Five things to try

1. **Hold Command over a link.** Move the pointer over a reference to an equation, figure, table or citation and hold ⌘ to [preview its target](link-peek.md). Let go and the preview closes.
2. **Search.** Press ⌘F. [Results](search.md) appear in the sidebar as they're found, and ⌘G steps through them.
3. **Save your place.** Select some text and press ⌘B to create an [anchor](anchors.md) you can jump back to.
4. **Mark it up.** Select text and press ⇧⌘H to [highlight it](text-markup.md), or press ⇧⌘A to show the Markup toolbar.
5. **Open two related documents as tabs.** Press ⌥⌘O to open the second one, then right-click a plain-text reference such as “Section 4.2 of the Master Agreement” to [find it in the other tab](cross-document-references.md).

## Find your way around

The menu bar, sidebar and toolbars cover most features, and right-clicking gives you the commands that fit what's under the pointer. See [The VectorPDF window](the-window.md) for a tour, [Right-click menus](right-click-menus.md) for what's in each menu, and [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md) for the full list.

To search this guide, use the search field at the top of this window.
