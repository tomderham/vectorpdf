# Tabs and windows

## Tabs

VectorPDF uses standard macOS window tabs.

- **New tab:** press ⌘T, or click the + button in the tab bar.
- **Open a PDF in a new tab:** press ⌥⌘O, or drag it onto an open document.
- **New window:** press ⌘N for an empty window, or ⌘O to open a PDF in a new window.
- **Move a tab:** drag it onto another window's tab bar, or out of the tab bar to give it a window of its own.
- **Switch tabs:** press ⇧⌘] for the next tab and ⇧⌘[ for the previous one (Go > Next Tab and Go > Previous Tab). ⌃Tab and ⇧⌃Tab work too.

To save the open tabs as a group, right-click the document's title, then choose Save All Open Tabs as a Group. See [Favorites and Tab Groups](favorites-and-tab-groups.md).

Keeping related documents as tabs of one window also lets VectorPDF [follow references between them](cross-document-references.md).

## Find an open window

The Window menu and the VectorPDF icon in the Dock list your open windows in the order you opened them, with the tabs of each one indented under it. A window with tabs has a line above and below it, and is shown by its number of tabs (or by the name of the [Tab Group](favorites-and-tab-groups.md) you opened it from); a checkmark marks its current tab. Choose a window or a tab to bring it to the front. To open a new window, right-click the VectorPDF icon in the Dock, then choose New Window.

Next to a window:

- A checkmark marks the front window.
- A diamond marks a minimized window.
- A dot marks a window with unsaved changes in one of its tabs.

Anchor windows aren't listed: they come to the front with the tab they were opened from.

## Anchor windows

You can open any place in a document in a small window of its own, to keep a figure or definition in view while you read:

- ⌘-click a link.
- Right-click a link, selection, place on the page, search result or outline entry, then choose **Open in New Window**.

These windows have a banner at the top. Click it to jump back to the place it shows. Click **Close all Child Windows** in the banner to close every window opened from the same document. They also close when you close the document they came from.

VectorPDF doesn't open a duplicate: if the place is already open in a window, it brings that window forward.
