# Draw, add notes, text and callouts

Press ⇧⌘A, or click the Markup Toolbar button, to show the Markup toolbar. Choose a tool, then a color and size. To choose a color, click the color button near the right of the toolbar, then click a color. To finish with a tool, press Escape or choose Text Selection Mode, the first tool in the toolbar.

## Draw freehand

Choose the pen tool, set the line width to Thin, Medium or Thick, then drag on the page.

## Draw a shape

1. Choose the Shapes tool, then choose Rectangle, Oval, Line or Arrow, or Revision Cloud.
2. Set the line width to Thin, Medium or Thick. For a line, use the Line Ending menu to choose an arrowhead, such as Open Arrow, Closed Arrow or Diamond, or None for a plain line.
3. Drag on the page. A rectangle or oval fills the area you drag across. A line runs from where you start to where you stop, with its ending at the end.

Hold ⇧ while you drag to draw a square or circle, or to keep a line at a multiple of 45°.

A revision cloud marks an area that has changed or needs review. Drag around the area, and the cloud follows the outline you draw. A quick drag straight across makes an oval cloud instead.

To select, move or right-click a rectangle, oval or cloud, use its outline, so you can still select the text inside it.

To change a line after you draw it, drag either end to move just that end, or select it and choose a different line width or line ending in the toolbar. You can also right-click it and choose Line Ending. Select a rectangle or oval and choose a line width to change its width.

## Add a text box

1. Choose the text box tool, and set a font size from 6 to 32 points.
2. Click the page where you want the text, then type.
3. Click outside the box, or press Escape, to finish.

## Add a callout

A callout is a text note with an arrow pointing to something on the page.

1. Choose the callout tool.
2. Press on the point you want the arrow to touch, then drag to where you want the note.
3. Type your note.

You can drag the arrow's target and the note separately later.

To choose what the line ends with, such as an open arrow, diamond or slash, use the Line Ending menu in the toolbar before you draw. To change an existing callout, right-click it and choose Line Ending.

## Add a sticky note

A note is a small icon on the page with a comment behind it.

1. Choose the sticky note tool.
2. Click where you want the note, then type.
3. Click away to finish. A note you leave empty is removed.

Double-click a note to read or change it.

## Reply to an annotation

Any annotation can have replies: a note, highlight, drawing, shape, cloud or measurement. Double-click it, or right-click it and choose Comments…, to see who made it and when. You can do this for annotations from other people and other apps too. From there you can:

- Type in the Reply field and press Return to add a reply.
- Use the Status menu to mark it Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled or Completed.
- Click the trash button to delete it with its replies.

Double-clicking a text box or callout edits its text instead; right-click it to reach its comments. For a line or drawing that came with the file, right-click it too.

Your name on annotations comes from Annotation Author in [Settings](settings.md).

## See all annotations

Click the Comments tab in the sidebar to list every annotation in the document by page, with its comment, status and replies. The tab appears once the document has an annotation. Click one to go to it on the page. Double-click it to open its comments there, to reply or change its status. Use the filter button at the top to show only one type, author, status or date.

For a table you can sort and filter in a larger window, click the Annotations button at the right of the Markup toolbar. Select one and click Show on Page, or double-click it, to go to its page.

## Change an annotation

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Edit text | Double-click a text box or callout, select it and press Return, or right-click it and choose Edit Text Box or Edit Callout Note |
| Move | Drag it, or select it and press the arrow keys. Hold ⇧ to move 10 points at a time. |
| Resize | Select it, then drag a corner handle. For a line or arrow, drag either end. |
| Delete | Select it and press Delete, or right-click it and choose the delete command, such as Delete Drawing or Delete Text Box |

Use the Eraser in the Markup toolbar to delete annotations by clicking or dragging across them. Deleting an annotation deletes its replies too.

### Annotations from other people and apps

You can change annotations that were already in the document, such as another reviewer's shapes, notes and clouds, the same way:

- Drag a rectangle, oval, cloud, line, note, stamp or text box to move it, or select it and press the arrow keys. It keeps its appearance, author, date and replies.
- Drag a corner handle of a rectangle, oval or cloud to resize it, or either end of a line to move that end.
- Select one and choose a color, line width or line ending in the Markup toolbar to change it. VectorPDF then draws it again in its own style.

Highlights, underlines, strikethroughs, freehand drawings and measurements from the file stay where they are, as yours do, but you can delete them or reply to them. You can't edit the text of a text box from another app.

## Undo a change

Choose Edit > Undo, or press ⌘Z, to take back adding, moving, restyling or deleting an annotation. Press ⇧⌘Z to redo it. This works for any annotation, including one that was already in the document and that you deleted, or erased with the Eraser by mistake. It comes back with its replies, author and date.

Your annotations are stored in the PDF as standard annotations when you [save](save.md), so they appear in other PDF apps, along with their authors, replies and statuses. To bake them into the page, [export a flattened PDF](export.md#flattened-pdf).
