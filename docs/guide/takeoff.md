# Takeoff table

The takeoff table is a schedule of every measurement in a document, with its page, type, value and scale, and totals for length and area.

Click **Takeoff Table** in the [Measurement toolbar](measure.md).

- **Copy Table** copies the table, ready to paste into a spreadsheet.
- **Export CSV** saves it as a file.

In a document with [layers](layers.md), the table also shows each measurement's layer, and leaves out measurements on hidden layers, so you can total one trade at a time.

Totals add lengths (dimensions and perimeters) and areas across all pages. Each measurement uses the scale of its page, and totals are shown in the units of the first measurement.
