# Rearrange pages

Page changes are made in the [Thumbnails](outline-and-thumbnails.md) view of the sidebar. They become part of the file when you [save](save.md).

## Select pages

Click a thumbnail. Hold ⌘ to add pages one at a time, or ⇧ to select a range.

## Move pages

Drag selected thumbnails to a new position. A line shows where they'll go. You can also right-click a page, then choose Move Page Up or Move Page Down.

## Other page commands

Right-click a thumbnail, or a selection of thumbnails, then choose:

- **Rotate Clockwise, Rotate Counterclockwise or Rotate 180°:** turns pages in the PDF itself, unlike the [Rotate buttons in the toolbar](zoom-and-layout.md#rotate-the-view).
- **Duplicate Page:** adds copies straight after the selected pages. You can also choose File > Duplicate Pages (⇧⌘D), which copies the selected thumbnails if they include the page you're on, or otherwise that page.
- **Insert Blank Page.**
- **Insert Pages from PDF:** adds pages from another PDF. You can also choose File > Insert Pages from PDF (⌥⌘I).
- **Save selected page as a PDF:** extracts pages into a new file.
- **Delete Page.**

## If you've made anchors

Adding, deleting or moving pages can leave an [anchor](anchors.md) pointing at the wrong place. VectorPDF warns you and offers to make the change in a copy instead.
