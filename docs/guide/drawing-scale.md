# Set a drawing scale

To get real-world measurements from a drawing, VectorPDF needs to know its scale. Press ⇧⌘M to show the Measurement toolbar, then click the scale button to open Drawing Scale Calibration.

## Use a standard scale

1. Under Standard Drawing Scales, choose the drawing type:
   - **Metric (ISO):** from 1:1 to 1:1000.
   - **Architectural (US):** such as 1/4" = 1'-0".
   - **Civil Engineering (US).**
2. Choose the scale.
3. Under Area Takeoff, choose the unit for areas.
4. Click Apply Scale.

The scale applies to all pages, and is stored in the PDF when you [save](save.md).

## Calibrate from a known length

If the drawing has a dimension you trust, or a scale bar:

1. Click **Calibrate by Measuring Known Line**.
2. Click the two ends of the line on the drawing.
3. Enter the line's real length, and choose its unit: feet and inches, decimal feet, inches, millimeters, centimeters or meters.
4. Click Apply Scale.

Use the longest known line you can, because small errors in clicking matter less over a long distance.
