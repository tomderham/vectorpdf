# Anchors

An anchor is a saved place in a document: a passage of text, an area of a page, or a target of a link. Anchors are kept separately from the PDF, and don't change it.

## Create an anchor

- Select text, or hold ⌥ and drag to select an area, then press ⌘B or choose Anchors > Add Anchor.
- Right-click a selection, link or place on the page, then choose **Create Anchor**.
- Right-click a [search result](search.md) or an [outline](outline-and-thumbnails.md) entry, then choose **Create Anchor**.

If nothing is selected, ⌘B creates an anchor for the current page.

**Create Anchor and Open in New Window** does the same and also shows the place in its own window.

## Use your anchors

Click the Anchors tab in the sidebar to see a card for each anchor, or use the Anchors menu.

- **Jump to an anchor:** click its card, or choose it from the Anchors menu.
- **See a larger preview:** hold the pointer over the card's thumbnail.
- **See anchors from every tab in the window:** choose All Tabs at the top of the Anchors tab. Click one to switch to that tab.

Right-click a card to open it in a new window, copy its text, or copy its image.

## Delete anchors

- **One anchor:** click the × on its card, or right-click it and choose Delete Anchor.
- **All anchors in a document:** click Clear All in the Anchors tab, or choose Anchors > Clear All Anchors.

## Anchors and page changes

An anchor remembers a page number. If you add, delete or move pages in a way that would leave an anchor pointing at the wrong place, VectorPDF warns you first. You can choose **Save as Copy** to make the change in a copy, which starts without anchors, or **Edit Anyway**.

## Anchors on your other Macs

Anchors [sync through iCloud](reading-positions.md) along with your reading position, unless you turn that off.
